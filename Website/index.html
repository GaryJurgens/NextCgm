How the Magic Happens

xDrip+ & The Cloud

1. Collect

xDrip+ acts as your local "command center," collecting raw data directly from your sensor via Bluetooth or as a companion app.

2. Transmit

Once xDrip+ has your readings, it securely beams them to your NextCGM cloud server.

3. Share

A live, "always-on" secure unique link allows you, your family, or doctor to view levels from any browser. It's a mobile-compatible website that can be downloaded as a PWA, giving you and your followers an "App-like feel".

Broad Compatibility

xDrip+ supports a massive range of CGMs, ensuring you can use the hardware you love with the cloud you deserve.

  • Dexcom: G6, G7, G4, G5, and Dexcom ONE.
  • Abbott Freestyle Libre: Libre 1, Libre 2 (EU), and Libre 3 (via companion apps).
  • Medtronic: Guardian sensors (via CareLink or companion bridge).
  • Linix CGM: Fully supported for seamless integration.
  • Other Sensors: Medtrum A6/A7, Eversense, Aidex, and Bluetooth-enabled meters.
